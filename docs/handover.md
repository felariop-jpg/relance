# Relance: your one-page guide

**What it is.** Relance helps you write personal follow-up emails to the people you work with, at the right time of year. It writes drafts with AI, and you read, change and send them yourself: Relance never sends anything.

**Your address:** **https://felariop-jpg.github.io/relance/**

Bookmark it in the browser you use every day, and always open Relance from that bookmark.

## The five things you do

1. **Open it** from your bookmark, on your usual computer and browser.
2. **See who's due** on **Due now**. If nobody is due this month, it shows the next batch. You can also click **Choose contacts yourself** to write to anyone.
3. **Generate:** tick the people, click **Generate drafts**, then confirm. Each email costs less than one euro cent.
4. **Review and edit** each draft on **Review**. Change anything you like, then click **Approve**. A draft with [WORDS IN SQUARE BRACKETS] needs a detail from you before you can approve it.
5. **Export and send:** click **Export** at the bottom of Review, then **Download**. Open the .zip file in your Downloads folder, then each email in it: Outlook shows it as a new message. Read it and press Send. Tick "contacted today" only if you send them today.

## Where your information lives

Your contacts and drafts are stored **only in this browser, on this computer**. They are not online, and nobody else can see them. Another browser or computer shows an empty Relance, and clearing your browsing history can erase them.

**Back up once a month:** **Settings → Backup → Download a backup**. Keep the file somewhere other than this computer too, such as a USB stick or your cloud storage. To bring everything back: **Settings → Backup → Restore from a backup**.

## If drafts stop working

- **"Your API key was not accepted"**, after it worked before: the key has probably expired. Create a new one with **Expires: Never** (see [Getting an API key](api-key.md)), then paste it into **Settings → AI connection**.
- **"Your Anthropic account has run out of credit"**: add credit at **platform.claude.com**, under Billing.
- **Relance is empty, or says it can't save anything:** you are in another browser, a private window or on another computer. Open your bookmark in your usual browser.

Nothing is lost in any of these cases: your contacts stay where they are.

## When the student hosting ends

Relance is hosted free through a student account, which will end one day. The address above may then stop opening. **Your contacts are not lost**: they are in your browser, not on the website. Your contact will either keep the same address (nothing for you to do) or give you a new one. On a new address, open **Settings → Backup → Restore from a backup** and choose your latest backup file. That's why a recent backup matters.
